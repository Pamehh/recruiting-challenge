## Q3 — A time you rejected AI output (or accepted bad output and changed your process)

> In the task of choosing where to host a frontend app. I went against Claude and GPT that recommended to keep the already in use at the company because I knew it was getting obsolete. Instead I went for a different implementation, since all I needed to store would be the form submissions and we already had a CRM I sent the replies to HubSpot and for the app I chose a free static host with the domain pointing at it. It was a nice approach because the platform was an urgent request and the times of setting up backend logic for a simple app would have taken longer plus I used the resources the company already had.
